# Images

Nexus page images, tracked in the repo. The background is 1300x372, the gallery images are 1920x1080. The mods-grid thumbnail is not a separate file: Nexus picks it from the uploaded gallery images. The images are made from 4K in-game screenshots of one save (day 76) on game 1.1.18293, with the tooltips cut out onto black. Each tooltip shows the final shape of the line: its own block at the end of the tooltip, with the rule above it. The text is Noto Sans (Bold for the name and the titles, Light for the subtitles), white plus the game gold `(232, 176, 48)`.

- `sl-item-totals-banner.jpg` - the mod page background image (1300x372): a repeating wallpaper of seven item tooltips, each with its `Total:` line, slightly turned and packed with no gap: Canned Luncheon Meat (trade window), Wild Three Delicacies (Perfect) (workbench window), Guinea Pig and Hedgehog (cooking window), Strawberry Seeds, Waste Plastic, and Basic Fertilizer (storage window).
- `sl-item-totals-thumb.jpg` - title card (1920x1080): "Item Totals" and "How many you have at home", beside a crop of a Medium Storage Rack full of seed bags with the Strawberry Seeds tooltip (`Total: 20`). Pick this as the mods-grid thumbnail.
- `sl-item-totals-tooltips.jpg` - "In every item tooltip" and "Storage · Trade · Cooking · Workbench" (1920x1080): five tooltips of four windows, overlapping only at empty corners: Canned Luncheon Meat `Total: 4` (trade window, below the Better Trade value), Guinea Pig `Total: 2` (cooking window, below the Project Cook tier and the trade value), Strawberry Seeds `Total: 20` and Waste Plastic `Total: 144` (storage window), and Wild Three Delicacies (Perfect) `Total: 2` (workbench window).
- `sl-item-totals-details.jpg` - "And in the item details" (1920x1080): the item detail popup of Strawberry Seeds, cut out, with a gold frame around `Total: 20` in its head line (`0.20kg · 1×1 · Total: 20`).

Not used: the screenshots with `Total: 0` (an item of a trader, and wire in a box on the floor), which read like a bug, and the screenshots of earlier builds that show the line in another place (the workbench rule drawn as a line of underscores, the popup line in the description, the trade line in the middle of the tooltip).
